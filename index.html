The day

Fresh air, big views, and the kind of day you remember.

Toby set out early, ready for a quiet climb and a little time away from the usual routine. The trail started cool and shaded, then opened into bright ridgelines with broad views over the valley.

By the time we reached the summit, the wind had picked up and the sky was glowing in warm gold and blue. It was the perfect place to pause, take in the views, and snap a few photos before heading back down.
